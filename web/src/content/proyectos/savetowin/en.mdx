# Nothing leaves this machine

saveToWin logs expenses and income on a server you own. Each account sees only its own data. The dashboard shows spending by category, the savings rate, and the budget against what actually happened. A transaction goes in by hand or from a bank CSV.

<Figura src="./img/portada.webp" alt="saveToWin sign-in screen, with the line that the account and the data stay on your server" />

<Nota>
Money is stored as whole cents. 1234 is €12.34. No floating decimals: the euro you see is only formatting.
</Nota>

It runs on Docker: a Node app (Hono and React) and PostgreSQL. The session lives in an httpOnly cookie, not in a loose token in the browser.
