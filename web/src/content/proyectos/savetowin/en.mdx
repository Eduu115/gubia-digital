## What it does

saveToWin is for logging expenses and income, sorting them, and seeing a dashboard: spending by category, savings rate, and budget against what actually happened. A bank CSV can be imported. Each account only sees its own data.

## How it is built

It runs on your own server with Docker: a Node app (Hono and React) and PostgreSQL. Money is stored in cents, not decimals, so the figures do not drift. Sign-in uses a hashed password and an httpOnly cookie.
